A reservation is a promise: a place, a time, and agreed terms. If a restaurant table becomes unusable before dinner, keeping that promise can mean changing several bookings together. This is Tablekeeper: keep the promise, even when the floor changes.

Behind it is Proofline, a software factory in BAND Desktop. Shivam Gupta directed the product and configured four coding-agent seats: Coordinator, Engineer, Experience and Verifier. The agents wrote and checked the implementation. Their standing mandates describe reusable responsibilities. Restaurant requirements belong in the task.

This is the real production room. One dispatch covered four stages. Engineer and Experience shared implementation work, while Verifier checked committed revisions independently. The handoffs connect decisions to the code and evidence they produced.

That separation caught something the published checks missed. In stage three, a corrupted restore could erase the permanent exception on an individually changed recurring booking.
