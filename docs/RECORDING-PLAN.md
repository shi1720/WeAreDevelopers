# Recording plan for Shivam Gupta

This document plans the recording. It does not certify that the build has passed or that a video exists. The final word-for-word script will use the actual accepted stage and evidence.

## Required footage

The official rules require a recording of the BAND Desktop room that produced the submitted code. A slide deck or a browser-only product tour does not replace that footage.

Record at 1920 x 1080 if available. Hide unrelated windows, notifications and private account settings. Keep the cursor deliberate. Capture BAND Desktop's actual submission room, then the locally running product. The submitted room ID is in `factory/run.json`. Preserve real handoffs, authors and review findings. Do not stage fake agent conversations.

The narration credits Shivam with product direction and factory setup, and the BAND seats with implementation and verification. This is both accurate and directly aligned with the autonomy criterion.

## Before recording

Use the completed `DEMO-RUNBOOK.md` from the experience seat for exact startup, synthetic accounts and scenarios. Avoid real user data. Confirm the room export matches the final run, the local app matches the accepted revision, and any displayed test counts are current. Speak naturally at roughly 135 words/minute. Leave two-second pauses between scenes for editing.

## Capturing with macOS

Use Shift-Command-5 to select the area or screen and choose the microphone in Options. Capture the BAND Desktop section while the actual room is visible. Use the same capture for the app demonstration, or make separate takes and join them. Avoid recording account credentials, API keys, private exports or terminal environment dumps. Save a clean original before editing. The product footage may be recorded without voice first, then narrated from the final script.

## Final export

Export MP4 at 1080p, check the entire video with audio, and make sure all text is legible. The video must show the BAND Desktop room, at least one real inter-seat handoff and the result. Upload to the platform required by the submission form, confirm judge-accessible permissions, then paste its URL into the form. Do not claim the video has been submitted until the form actually confirms it.
