Open https://tablekeeper-proofline.web.app and choose Start a private demo, then Create synthetic demo. No API key or shared password is needed. Use made-up information only; each browser session has isolated data and expires after two hours.

Three-minute test:
1. In Your bookings, open EVENING1. Note Window nook, 14 June 2035 at 19:00 Europe/London, two guests, policy 0.
2. Open Demo controls, Explore as manager. Load the roster for 14 June 2035.
3. Preview a Window nook closure from 19:00 to 21:00 that day. Expect one move among three considered bookings and zero unused seats. Preview does not apply changes.
4. Apply seating repair. The roster refreshes automatically and EVENING1 moves to Garden table.
5. Switch back to guest and inspect EVENING1. Arrival, party size and accepted terms are preserved; history records the reassignment.

Also try Find a table, booking edits, and Regular evenings. If a response is uncertain, Recover original result retries the saved request unchanged. Reset this demo affects only your synthetic space.
