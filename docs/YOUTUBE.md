Proofline + Tablekeeper: A BAND Agent Factory That Keeps Guest Promises

## Description

What happens when a restaurant table closes just before dinner?

Tablekeeper previews the smallest safe seating change while preserving guests' times and accepted terms. Proofline is the four-seat coding-agent factory in BAND Desktop that built and independently checked it for the WeAreDevelopers x BAND Dark Factory hackathon.

This walkthrough follows the real BAND collaboration, a review that rejected a flawed candidate, and the restaurant recovery workflow. Shivam Gupta directed the product and configured the factory.
